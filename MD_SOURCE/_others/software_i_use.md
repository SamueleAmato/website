# Software I Use

I prefer software that gets out of the way. My setup focuses on minimalism, speed, and keyboard-driven workflows. Here is a curated list of the programs I use daily.

## System & Environment

* **Operating System:** [Alpine Linux](https://alpinelinux.org/). Extremely lightweight, simple, and secure.
* **Window Manager:** [dwm](https://dwm.suckless.org/). Fast, minimal, and compiles in seconds.
* **Terminal Emulator:** [Alacritty](https://github.com/alacritty/alacritty). 
* **Shell:** bash.
* **Launcher:** [dmenu](https://tools.suckless.org/dmenu/). A ridiculously powerful, yet entirely minimalist launcher by suckless.org.

## Text Editing & Productivity

* **Text Editor:** [vim](https://www.vim.org/). The standard for fast, keyboard-centric text editing.

> **NOTE:** Lately, I've been experimenting with [Emacs](https://www.gnu.org/savannah-checkouts/gnu/emacs/emacs.html). I haven't drawn my final conclusions on it yet, but I am exploring and daily-driving it right now to see how it fits my workflow.

* **PDF Viewer:** [zathura](https://pwmt.org/projects/zathura/). Minimal, fast, and vim-like bindings.
* **Presentations:** [sent](https://tools.suckless.org/sent/). Another beautiful suckless tool that makes presentations directly from plain text files. Occasionally, I also use reveal.js paired with Emacs org-mode.

## Internet & Communications

* **Web Browser:** [Ungoogled Chromium](https://github.com/ungoogled-software/ungoogled-chromium). 
* **Chat:** [Signal](https://signal.org/it/) is my primary choice. I also use XMPP, and standard email when absolutely necessary.
* **File Synchronization:** [Syncthing](https://syncthing.net/). Decentralized, peer-to-peer file sync. Keeps my devices updated without relying on someone else's cloud.
* **Torrent Client:** [qBittorrent](https://www.qbittorrent.org/).

## Media & Graphics

* **Video / Audio Player:** mpv. Don't bother with anything else.
* **Image Viewer:** [sxiv](https://github.com/xyb3rt/sxiv). Simple X Image Viewer. Handles everything I need with vim-like controls.
* **Image Editor:** GIMP for heavier editing and modifications.
* **Wallpaper Manager:** [feh](https://feh.finalrewind.org/).

## Other

* **Cryptocurrency:** [Monero](https://www.getmonero.org/). True digital cash that is actually private and decentralized.
